abstract: |
  This chapter reviews multiple neuronal mechanisms for encoding time in the primate medial premotor cortex. Neural populations represent different aspects of single-interval and rhythmic timing through categorical signals, ramping activity, sequential activation patterns, mixed selectivity, and low-dimensional population dynamics. Together, these findings suggest that the medial premotor cortex contains multiple neural clocks that flexibly combine temporal information with other task variables.
