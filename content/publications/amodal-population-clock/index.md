abstract: |
  We investigated neuronal population activity in the medial premotor cortex of monkeys performing rhythmic tapping guided by visual or auditory metronomes. Across sensory modalities, neural activity followed circular trajectories that regenerated with each interval and converged near tapping times. Tapping tempo was represented through a combination of amplitude modulation and temporal scaling. Although sensory modality displaced the trajectories into visual- and auditory-related subspaces, the underlying timekeeping dynamics remained similar. These findings support an amodal neural clock in the medial premotor cortex that coordinates rhythmic tapping across sensory modalities.
